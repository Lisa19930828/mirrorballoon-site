About Us

Joyful Decoration is a professional design studio and factory specializing in inflatable decoration, especially inflatable mirror balloons and lighting inflatable models.

We supply full series of inflatable mirror balloon, decorative inflatable model, LED inflatable decoration, etc.

With years of experience in design and production, we have served customers from all over the world. Our products are widely used in events, parties, weddings, shopping malls, stages, and advertising displays.

We have professional designers and skilled workers to ensure high-quality products and creative designs. We also support custom size, color, logo printing according to your requirements.

Our mission is to provide safe, durable, beautiful and cost-effective inflatable products to make every event more wonderful and unforgettable.
